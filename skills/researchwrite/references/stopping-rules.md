# Stopping rules

Three revision rounds or two consecutive score improvements below 0.5 trigger
a strategy review, not task completion. If actionable defects remain, change
the repair strategy within the user's budget and authorization.

Missing key evidence or an unresolved substantive specialist conflict blocks
the dependent claims. Apply the global AGENTS completion rule to the remaining
work. Finish when the requested deliverable meets its acceptance criteria.

When stopping, report:

1. Current score/status
2. Remaining problems
3. Why the loop stopped
4. One recommended next action

Do not hide unresolved evidence gaps with smoother prose.
