# Journal: Nature Communications (writing)

## Read the shared facts first

Open `../../../../nature-shared/journal-formats/nat-comms.md` for the authoritative formatting facts: word limits, abstract rules, figure specs, reference style, mandatory statements, and common desk-rejection patterns.

The notes below are the **drafting action layer** on top of those facts.

## Audience

Open-access, broader than a subfield journal, more specialist-tolerant than *Nature*. The reader is typically an active researcher in an adjacent area.

## Drafting priorities

- Significance framing matters, but a less aggressive opening than *Nature* is acceptable.
- Tight prose is still preferred even though limits are looser than *Nature*.
- Methods can be more detailed in-line; reproducibility expectations are explicit.
- Same em-dash and hedging defaults as *Nature*.

## Word budget and author decisions

Use the Article limits and word-count scope in the shared facts; do not duplicate
a numeric limit table here. For a new full-manuscript plan that needs a word
allocation, propose the section budget and retain author confirmation before
committing to substantive allocation or cuts. Reuse an already approved budget.
A requested paragraph or section does not require a new whole-paper budget.

Methods is counted separately. Preserve sufficient replication detail; a
recommended main-text length does not authorize unrequested scientific cuts.

## Submission and display planning

For an authorized full submission package, identify the applicable data/code
availability, reporting forms, cover letter and display-item requirements from
the shared facts. Missing package metadata does not block an otherwise supported
standalone section. Preserve access restrictions and all publication approvals.

## Abstract drafting

Use the current shared maximum and an unreferenced, unstructured paragraph.
Define necessary abbreviations and communicate the main finding and implications.
Use supported quantitative results where helpful; do not invent them to fill a template.

## Cross-skill notes

If the user is transferring from a *Nature* draft, the most common rework is:
1. Verify the current Article structure and count Methods separately.
2. Reconcile display items with the current length-dependent guidance.
3. Adapt significance framing only within the requested scientific scope.
