# Language: Chinese writing

Draft in Chinese when Chinese is the requested output or the input is Chinese
and no translation was requested. Preserve established English technical terms,
identifiers, units, DOI and source locators where appropriate.

Use the same evidence, terminology and claim-strength rules as the writing
core. Match the discipline and the author's accepted prose; avoid mechanically
translating English templates or adding formulaic significance claims.

Chinese and English abstracts of the same work must agree on the question,
methods, numerical findings and scope; they need not have identical sentence
counts or word order. Produce both languages only when requested.
