# Language: Chinese source and Chinese output

Keep the output in Chinese. Do not translate the passage into English or force
English clause order onto otherwise clear Chinese academic prose.

## Sentence and paragraph rules

- Preserve technical terms, figures, units, citations, causal direction,
  modality, and the source's epistemic strength.
- Improve subject clarity, clause order, logical relations, rhythm, and
  concision without changing the argument or evidence boundary.
- Remove repetition, empty transitions, stacked nominalizations, and ambiguous
  pronouns only when doing so does not alter scientific meaning.
- Do not automatically add `可能`、`或许`、`初步`、`在一定程度上` or similar
  defensive qualifiers. Add or remove a hedge only when the source meaning or
  verified evidence boundary requires it.
- Do not inflate the prose with generic audit language, self-commentary, or
  claims about what has or has not been verified.
- In `LANGUAGE_ONLY`, keep paragraph functions and order fixed; surface a true
  contradiction separately rather than repairing it through prose.
