# Design provenance

This local contract was independently distilled on 2026-09-02 from two MIT-
licensed public skills and the existing Research Agent evidence-bound writing
contract:

- https://github.com/Adkid-Zephyr/anti-defensive-writing-Skill
- https://github.com/Kiterlin/anti-defensive-writing

Adopted ideas: contribution-forward openings, minimal targeted edits, removal
of work-log narration, sentence-function classification, positive scope, and
source-specific uncertainty.

Rejected ideas: hiding unfavourable results, changing metrics or comparison
frames after seeing results, deleting material experiments, treating negative
evidence as a favourable trade-off without support, global hedge deletion, and
story rebasing without an author decision.

The local wording is newly written. The upstream repositories remain the
canonical sources for their own text and licenses.
